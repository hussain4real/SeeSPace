# SeeSpace product context

Recorded 12 November 2026 for Phase 1 visual exploration. This context summarizes the existing plan and BRS; it does not approve unresolved policies or a visual identity.

## Register

product

## Users

Nigeria-first seekers browsing houses and land for rent or sale without an account. Publishers maintain their authorized inventory; moderators review publication separately. Team membership is not marketplace authority.

## Product purpose

Help a visitor find a relevant property, understand its purpose and price basis, and send a nonbinding enquiry. Preserve the existing Laravel/Inertia/Vue scaffold and real authentication, settings and teams. Private management is later scope.

## Brand personality

The source design brief proposes calm, credible and welcoming. Phase 1 explores spacious discovery versus compact comparison; neither treatment is approved. Clear information should earn trust without verification claims.

## Anti-references

Avoid finance-dashboard chrome on public pages, invented popularity counts, testimonials, urgency, verification badges, checkout, booking and P2/P3 teasers. The former Claude prototype is optional flow reference, not binding design. The workflow is now Codex only.

## Design principles

- Give Rent and Buy equal clarity; lead with discovery rather than registration.
- Keep house and land attributes appropriate, and sale totals distinct from rent periods.
- Separate public display information from private account and management records.
- Distinguish enquiry receipt, notification delivery and any later agreement.
- Preserve proposed versus confirmed requirements and stop at human selection gates.

## Accessibility and inclusion

The source brief proposes WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard access, visible focus, labelled fields, reduced motion, 44 px controls and 360/768/1440 px layouts. Static probes cannot prove these behaviors. Full prototype validation belongs to M2 after direction selection.
